                                             What Employers Need to Know:
                                            Premiums & Counting Employees

Maine's Paid Family and Medical Leave Law (PFML) applies to any employer who has at least one Maine
employee. Here's what employers need to know and actions to take in advance.

   1. Count your employees. Premium rates are determined by the number of Maine employees

         you had in the 12-month period before September 30th of each year. Within that period, count the
         number of employees that you paid wages to each week. This includes any employees who were
         on payroll for a calendar work week ­ including full time, part time, seasonal, or per-diem. If there
         are 15 or more Maine employees paid in 20 or more weeks, your premium rate will be 1.0% of
         wages and you may deduct up to half of the premium from your employees' wages. If the there are
         less than 15 Maine employees, your premium rate will be 0.5% of wages, and you may deduct all
         or a portion of the contribution from your employees' wages. Wages mean all compensation,
         including tips, gratuities, severance, terminal pay, commissions, and bonuses. Wages do not
         include payments to independent contractors.

         To determine if employment is reportable in Maine, the following criteria are used. If you answer
         "yes" to this series of questions, then the employment is subject to Maine PFML.
         1. Is the employee's work performed in Maine? If no, go to Question 2.
         2. Does the individual perform some work in Maine and the physical base of operations in Maine?

             If no, go to Question 3.
         3. Does the individual perform some work in Maine AND is the service directed and controlled

             from Maine?? If no, go to Question 4.
         4. Does the individual perform some work in Maine and live in Maine? If no, the individual is not a

             Maine employee.

   2. Withhold contributions. You may withhold up to 0.5% of your employees' wages so ensure

         your payroll system and/or payroll processor can accommodate. Be sure to inform your
         employees in advance that this will take place using the template notices and poster provided by
         the Maine Department of Labor. Premiums and wage reports are due 30 days after the end of the
         quarter and be submitted through the Maine Paid Leave Portal.

   3. Register in the Paid Leave Portal. All employers subject to the Maine PFML law are required

         to register in the Paid Leave Portal. If you use a payroll processor, they can request access to your
         account.

   4. Stay informed. Stay up to date on the latest information at Maine.gov/paidleave. Sign up for

         the "Get Notified" email list to get program and portal information as it is released.
