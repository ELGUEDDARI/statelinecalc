                                        FORMULA FOR COMPUTING SOUTH CAROLINA
                                                        2026 WITHHOLDING TAX

Employers must calculate taxable income for each employee. Then compute the amount of tax to be withheld using the
Subtraction Method or the Addition Method.

I. Compute annualized salary
    Multiply weekly salary by 52 weeks to calculate the annual gross wages.

II. Calculate taxable income                              Example for Standard Deduction
    Deduct from gross wages:
     Personal Allowance                                   Annualize salary: $750 per week, 3 allowances
        $0 if zero allowances claimed OR                       $ 750.00 per week
        $5,000 per personal allowance claimed                        X 52 weeks
                                                              39,000.00 gross wages
AND
     Standard Deduction                                     - 15,000.00 personal allowances (3 allowances X $5,000)
        $0 if zero allowances claimed OR                     - 3,900.00 standard deduction ($39,000 X 10%)
        10% of gross wages if claiming one or more           $20,100.00 taxable income
        allowances, up to $7,500 total

III. Calculate the Withholding Tax
    Use the Subtraction Method or the Addition Method to calculate the Withholding Tax based on the taxable income.

IV. Calculate weekly withholding
    Divide the Withholding Tax by 52 to calculate the amount to withhold from the employee each week.

Example: $549.90 DIVIDED BY 52 WEEKS = $10.58 STATE TAX PER WEEK
