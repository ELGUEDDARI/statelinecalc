$25 an Hour Is How Much a Year?

$25 an hour is $52,000 a year before tax, at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks. That is the figure every other page gives you, and it is the easy half of the question.

The half that decides what reaches your account is your state. On the same $52,000, Texas leaves you $43,962 and Illinois $41,533 — a gap of $2,429 a year, or $202.43 a month, on identical pay.

Questions

$25 an hour is how much a year?
$25 an hour is $52,000 a year before tax, at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks. After tax it depends entirely on where you work: Texas leaves you $43,962 and Illinois $41,533, a difference of $2,429 on identical pay.
$25 an hour is how much a year after taxes?
Between $41,533 and $43,962 depending on the state, for a single filer taking the standard deduction in 2026. That is an effective rate of 20.1 to 15.5 percent. The table above shows every state we publish, and each figure is the same arithmetic our paycheck calculator runs, not a rounded estimate.
$25 an hour is how much a month?
$4,333.33 a month before tax. After tax, between $3,461.07 and $3,663.50 a month depending on the state. Note that a month is not four weeks: twelve monthly payments of $4,333.33 and twenty-six biweekly payments of $2,000.00 come to the same $52,000.
$25 an hour is how much a week?
$1,000.00 a week before tax at 40 hours, and $2,000.00 for a biweekly pay period. Overtime, unpaid leave and short weeks all move this figure, which is why the annual total below assumes a full 2,080 hours.
Is 2,080 hours the right number to multiply by?
It is the convention -- 40 hours a week for 52 weeks -- and it assumes no unpaid time off. It is not the law. At 35 hours a week $25 an hour comes to $45,500 a year, and at 30 hours $39,000. The table of weekly hours above gives the honest range instead of one number.
Why does the yearly figure differ from other calculators?
Most pages answering this question multiply by 2,080 and stop. That is the gross figure, and it is not the number that reaches your account. This page also shows what is left after federal income tax, Social Security, Medicare and state withholding, which is why our after-tax figures are lower and closer to your bank statement.
Does the state really change the answer that much?
On $52,000 a year, the gap between Texas and Illinois is $2,429 a year, or $202.43 a month. It is the single largest variable in the answer after the hours you actually work.
