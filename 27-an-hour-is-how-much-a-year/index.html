$27 an Hour Is How Much a Year?

$56,160 a year, before tax

$27 an hour is $56,160 a year before tax, at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks. That is the figure every other page gives you, and it is the easy half of the question.

The half that decides what reaches your account is your state. On the same $56,160, Florida leaves you $47,305 and Illinois $44,669 — a gap of $2,635 a year, or $219.59 a month, on identical pay.

Where $27 an hour sits in the US labor market

Across all US occupations the median wage is $24.51 an hour and the mean is $33.54 — Bureau of Labor Statistics, Occupational Employment and Wage Statistics, May 2025 data covering 155,495,730 jobs, released May 15, 2026.

At $27 an hour you are about 10% above the national median. More than half of US jobs pay less than this. The national mean of $33.54 is still higher, but a mean is pulled up by the best-paid occupations rather than by typical pay.

Annualized, $56,160 compares with a national mean of $69,770 a year. The median and the mean differ because a minority of very high-paying occupations pulls the average up; the median is the better description of a typical job.

How close $27 an hour is to the next federal bracket

Most pages answering this question stop at $56,160. The number that decides what a raise is actually worth is a different one: where $56,160 falls in the federal brackets.

After the $16,100 standard deduction, $56,160 leaves $40,060 of taxable income for a single filer. That sits in the 12% federal bracket, which runs to $50,400 of taxable income. Only part of it is taxed at that rate: the first $12,400 is taxed at the lower brackets and only the last $27,660 at 12%. A bracket applies to the dollars above its threshold, never to the whole salary. The “effective rate” column in the table below measures something different again: the share of gross pay lost to every deduction together — federal income tax, Social Security and Medicare at 7.65% combined, and any state income tax. There is $10,340 of room left before the next dollar would be taxed at 22% — about 383 more hours at $27 an hour, or a raise of about $4.97 an hour held for a full year.

Questions

$27 an hour is how much a year?
$27 an hour is $56,160 a year before tax, at 40 hours a week for 52 weeks. After tax it depends entirely on where you work: Florida leaves you $47,305 and Illinois $44,669, a difference of $2,635 on identical pay.
$27 an hour is how much a year after taxes?
Between $44,669 and $47,305 depending on the state, for a single filer taking the standard deduction in 2026. That is an effective rate of 20.5 to 15.8 percent. The table above shows every state we publish, and each figure is the same arithmetic our paycheck calculator runs, not a rounded estimate.
$27 an hour is how much a month?
$4,680.00 a month before tax. After tax, between $3,722.45 and $3,942.05 a month depending on the state. Note that a month is not four weeks: twelve monthly payments of $4,680.00 and twenty-six biweekly payments of $2,160.00 come to the same $56,160.
$27 an hour is how much a week?
$1,080.00 a week before tax at 40 hours, and $2,160.00 for a biweekly pay period. Overtime, unpaid leave and short weeks all move this figure, which is why the annual total below assumes a full 2,080 hours.
Is 2,080 hours the right number to multiply by?
It is the convention -- 40 hours a week for 52 weeks -- and it assumes no unpaid time off. It is not the law. At 35 hours a week $27 an hour comes to $49,140 a year, and at 30 hours $42,120. The table of weekly hours above gives the honest range instead of one number.
Why does the yearly figure differ from other calculators?
Most pages answering this question multiply by 2,080 and stop. That is the gross figure, and it is not the number that reaches your account. This page also shows what is left after federal income tax, Social Security, Medicare and state withholding, which is why our after-tax figures are lower and closer to your bank statement.
Does the state really change the answer that much?
On $56,160 a year, the gap between Florida and Illinois is $2,635 a year, or $219.59 a month. It is the single largest variable in the answer after the hours you actually work.
